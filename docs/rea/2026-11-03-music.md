# Music (`A_PlayMusic`, `music.txt`)

Cues are top-level blocks in `data\database\music\music.txt` (inside `x_music.ras`). Each block gives
`Filename` (relative to `data\database\music\`), a `FadeInTime` and `Looping`. Of the cues, 31 are
`Looping = false`, among them `empty` (`wavs\empty.wav`). The rest loop. Source: retail text, VERIFIED.

## Who plays music

- **Modes:** `data\menu\menu.txt` `[OnInit]` binds `maxpayne_menumode->a_playmusic(max_payne)` and
  `maxpayne_graphicnovelmode->a_playmusic(graphic_novel)`. `globalconfig` `LevelOnInitMessages` stops the music
  when a level starts (`a_playmusic("")`). The engine follows both (`menu_ui::Music::play_mode`).
- **Level scripts:** they call `maxpayne_gamemode->A_PlayMusic(cue)`. That is 195 calls in the shipped
  levels, all sent to `maxpayne_gamemode`. The most frequent cues are `old_building` 42,
  `city_ambience` 16 and `skyscraper_rooftop_stormy` 15. `A_PlayMusic("")` stops the music.

## Engine

`A_PlayMusic` stores the last requested cue on the scene. The walkthrough loop then plays it through its
own `Music` player:
- A new cue replaces the one that is playing.
- An empty cue stops the music.
- `Looping` is honoured per cue.
- The player stops when the level ends.

## Open

- `FadeInTime` / fade-out between cues: cues cut instead.
- **Handler not read:** that a new cue replaces the old one is a HYPOTHESIS. It rests on the verb's
  name and on the mode bindings.
